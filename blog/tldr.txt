<h2>tl;dr</h2>

<p>

We present IASC, an <em>Interactive Agentic System for ConLangs</em>
(constructed languages). We use the system as a benchmark for probing the
metalinguistic abilities of Large Language Models.

</p>

<p>
Fun fact: <em>iasc</em> is <em>fish</em>/魚/<em>sakana</em> in Irish.
</p>
